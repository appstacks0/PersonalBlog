Fried Chicken

Introduction

Recipe Information

  • Prep: 30 mins
  • Cook: 25 mins
  • Total: 55 mins
  • Servings: 4
Nutrition Facts (Per serving)
Energy 320kcal
Protein 28g
Carbohydrates 15g
Fat 18g

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken wings and drumsticks
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon five-spice powder
  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Fresh herbs for garnish (cilantro, mint)

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, combine chicken with garlic, fish sauce, soy sauce, black pepper, and five-spice powder. Marinate for at least 2 hours or overnight.
  2. Remove chicken from marinade and pat dry with paper towels.
  3. Coat each piece of chicken thoroughly in cornstarch.
  4. Heat oil in a deep fryer or large pot to 350°F (175°C).
  5. Fry chicken in batches for 12-15 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Drain on paper towels and garnish with fresh herbs before serving.

Cook's Note

For extra crispy chicken, double-fry the pieces: first at 325°F for 8 minutes, then let rest for 5 minutes, and fry again at 375°F for 4-5 minutes until golden brown.
